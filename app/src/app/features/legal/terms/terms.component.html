FundPath is a funding-discovery tool for Utah startups. By using FundPath you agree to the following terms.

The service

FundPath analyzes a description of your company against a corpus of government funding programs and returns a ranked, explained path of programs you may be eligible for. FundPath does not submit applications on your behalf and does not guarantee funding — it's a research aid, not a grant writer or a broker.

FundPath Alerts — messaging program

If you opt in, FundPath Alerts sends your funding path summary and lets you know when newly published programs match your profile, by email and/or text message.

Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. See our Terms and Privacy Policy.

Opting in is never required to use FundPath — you can build and view your funding path without providing an email or phone number.
