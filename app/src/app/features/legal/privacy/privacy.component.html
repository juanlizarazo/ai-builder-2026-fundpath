Privacy Policy

Last updated August 2026

FundPath helps Utah startups find government funding programs they're eligible for. This page explains what information we collect, why we collect it, and how we handle it.

What we collect

When you use FundPath, we collect the company description you submit (industry, size, revenue, funding need) so we can match you to relevant programs. If you choose to receive updates, we also collect the email address and/or phone number you provide, and whether you consented to text messages and when.

Why we collect it

Your company description is used only to build your funding route and to check for newly published programs that match your profile. Your email and phone number are used only to send you your funding route summary and to alert you when new programs match — nothing else.

Messaging data

Mobile information will not be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes. Text messaging originator opt-in data and consent will not be shared with any third parties.

Retention and deletion

We keep your profile and route data for as long as your account is active so you can return to your route. To request deletion of your data, email us at privacy@fundpath.dev and we'll remove it.

Contact

Questions about this policy: privacy@fundpath.dev.
